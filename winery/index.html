Showcase for wineries, with an invented demo winery. Enter the password you were given.

Privacy

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and operations, see GitHub's privacy statement.

The site sets no cookies, has no tracking or analytics and loads nothing from other servers: fonts and photo come from this site.

What you do here stays in this browser on this device: the key if you choose “Remember”, the language, and what you add or change in the demo (lists, shares, ratings). None of it is sent to us.

If you allow your location (distances in Explore), it is used only in your browser.

Links to shops and wineries open their websites; their privacy rules apply there.
